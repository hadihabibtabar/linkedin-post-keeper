# Privacy Policy

**LinkedIn Post Keeper** does not collect, transmit, sell or share any data.

## What the extension reads

While you browse `https://www.linkedin.com/`, the extension reads the posts displayed in your feed: the author's name, the post text and the post link. It only reads what is already shown to you in your own browser.

## Where it is stored

Saved posts are kept in `chrome.storage.local`, inside your own browser profile on your own computer. Nothing is sent to the developer or to any third party. The extension makes no network requests and uses no analytics, ads or tracking.

## Your control

- Open the extension popup and choose **Clear archive** to delete everything it has saved.
- **Export JSON** gives you a copy of your data.
- Removing the extension deletes its stored data.
- The extension keeps at most the 5,000 most recent posts.

## Permissions

- `storage` and `unlimitedStorage`: to keep the local archive.
- Access to `https://www.linkedin.com/*`: to read the feed and to stop it from reloading when you return to the tab.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published in this repository.
